## 🛠️ Motion Design & Editing Notes (For Video Creator)
1. **Screen Frame:** Use a floating, rounded browser window (border-radius 16px, subtle drop shadow `0 25px 50px -12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.25)`).
2. **Cursor Motion:** Smooth Bezier curves for all cursor movement; avoid jerky hand movements.
3. **Pacing:** Never show real-time waiting; jump-cut the 1-2 second AI generation so text appears to stream instantly.
4. **Captions:** Hardcode sleek kinetic captions at the bottom center (Plus Jakarta Sans, bold, white on dark rounded pill).
