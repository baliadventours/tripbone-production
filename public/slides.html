Idea Brief

Brief About the Idea Core Concept

Tripbone is a cloud-native SaaS that provides independent tour and private car rental operators with an enterprise-grade digital storefront and autonomous AI workforce—democratizing direct commerce.

🤖 8 Autonomous AI Tools

Powered by Google Gemini 3.8 Flash & 3.1 Flash Lite: instant tour creation, OTA catalog importer, dynamic travel planner with embedded tour inventory, 24/7 Search-grounded concierge, and email extractor.

🚗 Dual Tour & Car Rental Engine

Native management of scheduled day tours, multi-day packages, and private car rentals & chauffeur charters (with-driver vs. self-drive rates, island delivery zones & surcharges).

💳 Universal BYOPG (0% Commission)

Operators connect their own Stripe, Midtrans QRIS, Xendit, and PayPal accounts. 100% of customer payments settle directly into the operator's merchant bank account with zero platform take-rate.

Market Opportunity

Opportunities & Market Differentiation Why Now

1. How is it Different?

  • Legacy Engines (Bókun, FareHarbor): Clunky desktop interfaces, take 2–6% booking cuts, zero native Gen AI.
  • Generic CMS (Shopify, Wix): Lack travel logistics (pickup zones, tiered group rates, vehicle fleet specs, guide dispatch).
  • Tripbone: First unified platform combining an Airbnb-grade booking UX, dual tour & car rental engines, autonomous Gemini agents, and 0% commission.

2. How Does it Solve it?

  • Speed: Reduces tour & fleet setup time from 45 minutes to 2 seconds with Gemini 3.8 Flash.
  • Revenue: Eliminates the 25% OTA cut, boosting operator net margins by 20–30%.
  • 24/7 Capture: AI Concierge answers midnight questions on pickup and safety in under 2 seconds.

3. Core USPs

  • Catalog-Grounded AI Itineraries: Gemini embeds real, active bookable tour cards directly into custom itineraries.
  • Dual Tour & Car Rental Suite: Full car fleet management alongside scheduled tour packages.
  • Zero-Data-Entry Dispatch: Parses raw emails into verified QR check-in vouchers.
  • Universal Multi-Gateway BYOPG: Direct local currency settlement with no middleman.

Technology Stack

Technologies Used in the Solution Tech Stack

Google Cloud & AI Core

  • Google Gemini 3.8 Flash & 3.1 Flash Lite: Multimodal Gen AI for trip planning, tour generation, email extraction, and Search-grounded concierge.
  • Google Cloud Run: Serverless container runtime hosting the production Node.js API.
  • Firebase Firestore: Real-time NoSQL database with multi-tenant scoping.
  • Firebase Auth: Multi-role RBAC (SuperAdmin, Admin, Guide, Guest).
  • Google Maps Platform: Directions, Distance Matrix & Geocoding.

Frontend & UI

  • React 18 & TypeScript: Type-safe frontend.
  • Vite: Blazing fast compilation.
  • Tailwind CSS: Swiss-modern design system.
  • Lucide React & Motion: Kinetic UI icons & animations.

Payments & Universal BYOPG

  • Multi-Gateway Layer: Stripe, Midtrans QRIS, Xendit, PayPal, Razorpay, Adyen.
  • Zero Platform Take-Rate: 100% direct merchant payout.
  • WhatsApp Gateway API: Instant crew & guide dispatch.
